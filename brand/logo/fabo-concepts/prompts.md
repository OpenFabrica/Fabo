## 公共前缀

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished original logo proposal for "Fabo", a lightweight local container and Linux development app by OpenFabrica. Square canvas, truly transparent background. A single bold distinctive symbol centered above the exact wordmark "Fabo" (capital F, lowercase abo), beautifully kerned contemporary rounded sans serif, dark charcoal wordmark. Symbol should be readable as a small desktop app icon, simple silhouette, precise clean vector-like edges, flat colors, generous negative space. No slogans, no other text, no watermark, no mockup, no gradients, no shadows, no tiny decorative details. The result is a real usable isolated logo, not a presentation board.

## 01-open-container.png

Direction 1: An ingenious minimal open container / folded cube symbol, with its negative space subtly suggesting the letter F. Use two or three interlocking broad planes, a welcoming open top, a balanced compact geometric construction. Teal green and deep ocean blue. Friendly, dependable, quietly technical. Avoid shipping-container corrugations, generic cloud symbols, and literal Docker whale imagery.

## 02-modular-f.png

Direction 2: A memorable compact uppercase F monogram constructed from three softly rounded modular blocks, suggesting containers fitting together. Strong simple silhouette and clever deliberate negative space. Warm vivid orange with one deep rust accent, charcoal wordmark. Architectural precision with playful friendliness, visually distinct and recognizable at 24px. No cube, no hexagonal badge, no arrows, no cloud.

## 03-folded-ribbon.png

Direction 3: A minimal elegant folded ribbon forming a small open box and a subtle upward wing, representing a lightweight fast local runtime. One continuous broad rounded geometric ribbon, rich violet with a periwinkle accent. Compact, calm, premium, approachable. Keep the silhouette very simple, almost a custom typographic ligature, not a complex origami illustration. No cloud, no rocket, no speed lines, no container stacks.
